Capacity

Average per working day

Working days are Monday to Friday, plus any weekend day with activity. Net per day is pallets received minus pallets shipped. Days to capacity divides the open positions by that net rate, so it moves as receiving and shipping pick up or slow down. Today is left out of the averages because it isn't finished yet.

Estimated fill level by day

Worked back from today's inventory using the receipts and shipments for each day. Inventory adjustments outside of loads aren't included, so earlier days are estimates.
